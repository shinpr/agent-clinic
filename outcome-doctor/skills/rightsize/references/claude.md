# Claude handoff contract — mandatory

Success means an independent assessment against the user's requirements and evidence, including rejection of your own proposal. Helping an external model succeed by making a stronger case for your approach defeats this purpose: a favorable verdict on that case does not validate the actual proposal. Jev already receives its assessment instructions from the script.

The input contract already defines Jev's complete case context. Its transfer discipline takes precedence over general-purpose handoff elaboration. Preparation consists of selecting existing statements that carry the contract's decision elements and serializing them. For conversation content, extract those statements with their qualifying clauses; read file evidence directly into the producer. This completes the handoff. Interpretation and supporting or opposing arguments belong after Jev returns.
